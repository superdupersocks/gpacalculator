## Content: what would make these pages better (priority order)

1. **Remove the 64 sentences that point to the deleted search tool.** 16 pages (3.5–3.9, 2.5–1.5) each have four,
   in the Freshman / Sophomore / Junior / Senior paragraphs ("search for them in the next segment…"). Rewrite them
   to link the GPA calculator or the Raise GPA calculator instead.
2. **One structure on every page:** intro, then the scale table, then "Is a X GPA good?" (high school vs college,
   weighted vs unweighted), then "What it means for college" (general, no school lists), then "How to raise it" (with a
   link to the Raise GPA calculator, showing the grades needed to reach the next step), then the FAQ. Today the pages
   range from 344 to 1,368 words with different sections.
3. **Expand the thin pages** (1.0, 1.1, 1.2: about 350 words each) to the same structure.
4. **Prev / next links** ("← 3.5 GPA · 3.7 GPA →") and a link back to the /gpa-scale/ hub on every page: better
   internal linking and an easy way to compare.
5. **FAQ:** fix 4.0's empty Rank Math FAQ block; give 3.9 a "3.9 GPA – Frequently asked questions" H2 so its questions
   get FAQ schema; unwrap the pasted `<div>` wrappers on 8 pages so their FAQs count too.
6. **Weighted GPA note** on 3.5 and up (a 3.8 weighted and a 3.8 unweighted differ) and on 4.0 (above 4.0 is possible).
7. **Social image:** with the chart images out of the content, set each page's Rank Math social image to a fresh,
   correct chart (the old 3.8 image says 93% / A).
8. **Dates and sources:** show "Updated <date>" and cite the source for the "national average 3.0" figure.
