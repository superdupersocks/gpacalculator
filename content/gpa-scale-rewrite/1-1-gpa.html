Is a 1.1 GPA good?

No. A 1.1 is well below average: D work, about 66%, against a national average of 3.11 for 2019 graduates (NAEP High School Transcript Study). It is under the 2.0 that many colleges set as a minimum, so raising it is the priority.

In high school

Most four-year colleges will not admit a student below 2.0. Use the time you have left in high school to raise it; community colleges are open-admission and a common route to a four-year degree.

In college

In college a GPA below 2.0 usually means academic probation, which can affect financial aid. Talk to an academic advisor about a plan.

Weighted or unweighted?

These figures are for the unweighted 4.0 scale. If your school gives extra points for Honors, AP or IB classes, your weighted GPA can be higher than 1.1. Colleges usually recalculate GPAs their own way, so they see both. Work out yours with the Weighted GPA calculator.

What a 1.1 GPA means for college

With a 1.1 most four-year colleges will not admit you directly. Community colleges are open-admission, and many students transfer to a four-year college after earning good grades there.

If you are still in high school, every semester of better grades counts. Ask your counselor about credit recovery for classes you failed.

What helps

  • Start with your counselor: ask about credit recovery and retaking failed classes, which many schools replace or average.
  • Pass every class this term: an F counts as 0.0, so moving any F to a C makes a big difference.
  • Get support early: tutoring, after-school help or a study partner, from the first weeks of the term.

1.1 GPA – Frequently asked questions

What does a 1.1 GPA indicate?

A 1.1 GPA indicates very below-average academic performance, typically reflecting 'D' grades in most courses. This GPA suggests critical academic challenges and a pressing need for targeted interventions, guidance, and support to improve understanding and performance in coursework.

Is a 1.1 GPA good enough for college admissions?

A 1.1 GPA is significantly below the admissions standards for the vast majority of colleges, presenting major hurdles to gaining entry through conventional admission processes. Some institutions might have special programs or pathways for students demonstrating the potential for improvement. Strengthening your application with extracurricular activities, evidence of personal growth, and supportive recommendations is essential for showcasing your capabilities beyond academic performance.

How can I improve a 1.1 GPA?

Improving a 1.1 GPA involves a determined effort to secure better grades in future classes and, where possible, retaking classes with previously poor performance to enhance your academic record. Establishing effective study habits, utilizing academic support resources like tutoring, and prioritizing your academic goals are critical steps towards recovery and improvement.

What scholarship opportunities are available with a 1.1 GPA?

Opportunities for scholarships based on a 1.1 GPA may be limited, particularly those focused on academic merit. However, scholarships that consider broader life experiences, personal achievements, or characteristics such as community service, leadership qualities, or overcoming significant obstacles may be accessible. Some organizations and educational institutions offer scholarships aimed at students who show a strong commitment to turning their academic situation around, valuing determination and the potential for success despite initial academic struggles.
