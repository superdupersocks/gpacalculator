Is a 1.2 GPA good?

No. A 1.2 is well below average: D+ work, about 67%, against a national average of 3.11 for 2019 graduates (NAEP High School Transcript Study). It is under the 2.0 that many colleges set as a minimum, so raising it is the priority.

In high school

Most four-year colleges will not admit a student below 2.0. Use the time you have left in high school to raise it; community colleges are open-admission and a common route to a four-year degree.

In college

In college a GPA below 2.0 usually means academic probation, which can affect financial aid. Talk to an academic advisor about a plan.

Weighted or unweighted?

These figures are for the unweighted 4.0 scale. If your school gives extra points for Honors, AP or IB classes, your weighted GPA can be higher than 1.2. Colleges usually recalculate GPAs their own way, so they see both. Work out yours with the Weighted GPA calculator.

What a 1.2 GPA means for college

With a 1.2 most four-year colleges will not admit you directly. Community colleges are open-admission, and many students transfer to a four-year college after earning good grades there.

If you are still in high school, every semester of better grades counts. Ask your counselor about credit recovery for classes you failed.

What helps

  • Start with your counselor: ask about credit recovery and retaking failed classes, which many schools replace or average.
  • Pass every class this term: an F counts as 0.0, so moving any F to a C makes a big difference.

1.2 GPA – Frequently asked questions

What does a 1.2 GPA indicate?

A 1.2 GPA demonstrates very below-average academic performance, corresponding to 'D' grades across the majority of courses. This GPA level signals significant struggles with coursework and an urgent need for comprehensive academic support and personalized strategies for improvement.

Is a 1.2 GPA good enough for college admissions?

A 1.2 GPA falls considerably below the standard admissions criteria for most colleges, creating substantial barriers to traditional college admission. Nevertheless, certain colleges may provide alternative admission routes or support programs aimed at students with potential for academic growth. Enhancing your application with evidence of extracurricular achievements, personal development efforts, and recommendations that attest to your resilience and potential can be beneficial.

How can I improve a 1.2 GPA?

To improve a 1.2 GPA, prioritize earning higher grades in upcoming courses and retaking previously failed or low-performing classes to improve your academic record. Essential to this effort are establishing solid study habits, seeking out extensive academic support or tutoring, and committing fully to your educational improvement.

What scholarship opportunities are available with a 1.2 GPA?

While a 1.2 GPA limits access to scholarships based on academic merit, there are still financial aid options that consider other criteria. Scholarships that value community service, leadership experiences, personal perseverance, or unique skills can be more accessible. Additionally, certain scholarships are dedicated to students committed to overcoming academic challenges, recognizing the importance of growth, determination, and future potential over past academic performance.
