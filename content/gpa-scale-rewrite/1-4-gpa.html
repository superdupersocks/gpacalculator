Is a 1.4 GPA good?

No. A 1.4 is well below average: D+ work, about 69%, against a national average of 3.11 for 2019 graduates (NAEP High School Transcript Study). It is under the 2.0 that many colleges set as a minimum, so raising it is the priority.

In high school

Most four-year colleges will not admit a student below 2.0. Use the time you have left in high school to raise it; community colleges are open-admission and a common route to a four-year degree.

In college

In college a GPA below 2.0 usually means academic probation, which can affect financial aid. Talk to an academic advisor about a plan.

Weighted or unweighted?

These figures are for the unweighted 4.0 scale. If your school gives extra points for Honors, AP or IB classes, your weighted GPA can be higher than 1.4. Colleges usually recalculate GPAs their own way, so they see both. Work out yours with the Weighted GPA calculator.

What a 1.4 GPA means for college

With a 1.4 most four-year colleges will not admit you directly. Community colleges are open-admission, and many students transfer to a four-year college after earning good grades there.

If you are still in high school, every semester of better grades counts. Ask your counselor about credit recovery for classes you failed.

What helps

Now that you know where you stand it’s a good idea to think about ways to raise your GPA.  

  1. Start Early: It’s a good idea to check your grades regularly.  It takes time to raise a 1.4 GPA so you’ll want to start early.  Set a calendar reminder to check your grades each week and see how you’re progressing.    
  2. Make Time To Study: Set aside time in your schedule to study for each subject.  You may want to join a study group or even hire a tutor.  Having this time set aside regularly will help you stay on track and on top of improving your GPA.    
  3. Focus on Your Lowest Grades: It can feel overwhelming to raise your GPA so find your lowest grades and start there.  If you’re not sure how to raise your grades in those classes get in touch with your teacher and ask for suggestions.    

1.4 GPA – Frequently asked questions

What does a 1.4 GPA indicate?

A 1.4 GPA indicates significantly below-average academic performance, typically reflecting grades in the 'D' range across most courses. This GPA level suggests major difficulties in meeting coursework requirements and a pressing need for substantial academic support and improvement strategies.

Is a 1.4 GPA good enough for college admissions?

A 1.4 GPA is well below the standard criteria for the majority of colleges, presenting substantial challenges for gaining admission through standard processes. However, certain institutions might offer admission through alternative programs aimed at students with potential for improvement. To enhance your application, it's crucial to highlight areas of personal development, involvement in extracurricular activities, and secure recommendations that speak to your abilities and commitment to grow.

How can I improve a 1.4 GPA?

To improve a 1.4 GPA, it's essential to focus on significantly enhancing your grades in future courses. Retaking previously failed or low-performing courses can also help improve your GPA. Implementing comprehensive study plans, seeking extensive academic support, and prioritizing educational goals are necessary steps for academic recovery and success.

What scholarship opportunities are available with a 1.4 GPA?

While a 1.4 GPA significantly limits access to traditional academic scholarships, there may be financial aid opportunities that consider broader life experiences and personal achievements. Scholarships focusing on community service, leadership efforts, overcoming adversity, or specific talents can provide avenues for support. Some scholarships are particularly aimed at students who demonstrate a strong desire and plan for academic improvement despite past challenges.
