Is a 2.4 GPA good?

A 2.4 is below average. It works out to C+ work, about 79%, against a national average of 3.11 for 2019 graduates (NAEP High School Transcript Study). It meets the minimum at some colleges but leaves little room.

In high school

Many four-year colleges look for at least a 2.5, so this GPA limits your choices. Community colleges and open-admission schools remain open to you while you work on it.

In college

In college you are in good standing (usually 2.0 or above), but some majors, scholarships and graduate programs require more.

Weighted or unweighted?

These figures are for the unweighted 4.0 scale. If your school gives extra points for Honors, AP or IB classes, your weighted GPA can be higher than 2.4. Colleges usually recalculate GPAs their own way, so they see both. Work out yours with the Weighted GPA calculator.

What a 2.4 GPA means for college

A 2.4 meets the minimum at some four-year colleges and at all open-admission and community colleges. Many colleges look for 2.5 or higher, so your choices are limited for now.

Raising your GPA before you apply, or starting at a community college and transferring after a strong first year, opens more options.

Freshman

Most of high school is still ahead of you, so a 2.4 now can change a lot. Every semester of better grades pulls your average up; the table below shows how many strong classes it takes to reach a 2.5.

Sophomore

You are about halfway through, and raising a 2.4 is still realistic. Focus first on the classes where you are closest to the next grade, and turn in every assignment. Colleges also notice an upward trend.

Junior

Junior-year grades are the last full year most colleges see before you apply, so they count. Even if your overall GPA moves only a little, strong junior grades show an upward trend. Ask your counselor about retaking classes you failed.

Senior

Your GPA will not move much before applications, so plan around it: apply to colleges whose requirements you meet, including community colleges, and keep your senior grades up, because colleges see them and some offers depend on them.

What helps

Now that you know what a 2.4 GPA really means you may want to think about what you can do to raise your 2.4 GPA.  

  1. Prioritize: Students often have a lot going on, but if you want to raise your GPA you need to make studying a priority.  Set aside time for school work before socializing, sports or anything else.     
  2. Tackle Your Hardest Class: A single low grade can really hurt your GPA so spend extra time and attention on your hardest class.  You may even want to touch base with your teacher to find out what you can do to bring up your lowest grade.    
  3. Study Smart: If you’re having trouble getting good grades you may want to work with a counselor, teacher or teacher aid to get some study tips.  Changing the way you study can make a big difference in your success!

2.4 GPA – Frequently asked questions

What does a 2.4 GPA indicate?

A 2.4 GPA indicates a 'C+' to 'C' average, showing that your academic performance is around the average range. This GPA suggests that you have a foundational understanding of your coursework but also indicates considerable areas for academic development and growth.

Is a 2.4 GPA good enough for college admissions?

A 2.4 GPA meets the admission requirements for various colleges, especially those that adopt a holistic approach to evaluating applicants. While it may not suffice for more selective colleges, emphasizing other aspects of your application, such as extracurricular involvement, personal achievements, and strong letters of recommendation, can help offset the GPA and bolster your chances of admission.

How can I improve a 2.4 GPA?

Improving a 2.4 GPA requires a focused effort on earning better grades in your upcoming courses and possibly retaking classes where your performance was not strong. Effective strategies include enhancing your study habits, seeking academic support and tutoring, and dedicating sufficient time to your studies to raise your academic standing.

What scholarship opportunities are available with a 2.4 GPA?

While a 2.4 GPA may limit access to some competitive academic scholarships, there are scholarships available that consider a broader set of qualifications. Scholarships based on community service, leadership experiences, special talents, or overcoming challenges may be more accessible. These scholarships recognize diverse strengths and personal achievements beyond solely academic metrics.
