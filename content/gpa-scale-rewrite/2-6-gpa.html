Is a 2.6 GPA good?

A 2.6 is a little below average. It means B− work, about 81%, while 2019 high school graduates averaged 3.11 (NAEP High School Transcript Study). It is not a problem for many colleges, but it narrows your options at selective ones.

In high school

It meets the minimum at many four-year colleges, but you will be below the typical admitted student at selective ones. Improving your grades this year is worth the effort.

In college

In college it is under the 3.0 that many scholarships and graduate programs require, but well above the 2.0 needed for good standing.

Weighted or unweighted?

These figures are for the unweighted 4.0 scale. If your school gives extra points for Honors, AP or IB classes, your weighted GPA can be higher than 2.6. Colleges usually recalculate GPAs their own way, so they see both. Work out yours with the Weighted GPA calculator.

What a 2.6 GPA means for college

With a 2.6 you can get into many four-year colleges, especially regional public universities and less selective private colleges. Selective colleges are a stretch.

Colleges notice an upward trend, so better grades this year help. Community college followed by a transfer is another proven route.

What helps

Now that you know where you stand it’s a good idea to come up with a plan to raise your 2.6 GPA so that you can achieve your goals.    

  1. Two Heads are Better Than One: If you’re just not getting the grades you hope for even though you’re working hard.  Maybe it’s time to study smarter.  Join a study group, work with a tutor or ask a teacher’s aid for help.         
  2. Focus your Efforts: Even a single low grade can really bring down your GPA so give extra time and attention to your hardest class.  You may even want to check in with your teacher to find out what you can do to bring your grade.    
  3. On Time Every Time: Making sure you turn in every single assignment complete and on time is an easy way to guarantee some points.  Mark down due dates on your calendar and commit to turning in every assignment complete and on time every time.  

2.6 GPA – Frequently asked questions

What does a 2.6 GPA indicate?

A 2.6 GPA signifies a 'B-' to 'C+' average, showing that your grades are a mix between somewhat below average to average in your courses. It points out that you have a basic to moderate understanding of your coursework, with a clear opportunity for academic enhancement.

Is a 2.6 GPA good enough for college admissions?

A 2.6 GPA is acceptable for admission into a number of colleges, especially those that evaluate applicants on a holistic basis. While this GPA may not meet the standards of more selective institutions, a well-rounded application featuring solid extracurricular activities, personal essays that convey your story compellingly, and positive letters of recommendation can bolster your chances.

How can I improve a 2.6 GPA?

Improving a 2.6 GPA involves a concerted effort to score higher in your remaining courses and, if possible, retaking classes where you've previously earned lower grades. Key strategies for GPA improvement include refining your study habits, taking advantage of academic resources like tutoring, and efficiently managing your time to prioritize academic tasks.

What scholarship opportunities are available with a 2.6 GPA?

While a 2.6 GPA may limit eligibility for certain highly competitive academic scholarships, there are still many scholarship opportunities that consider a broader range of criteria. Scholarships focused on community involvement, leadership experiences, specific talents, or those targeting students who have overcome significant obstacles are accessible. These opportunities recognize the value of diverse achievements and personal qualities beyond just academic performance.
