Is a 2.7 GPA good?

A 2.7 is a little below average. It means B− work, about 80–82%, while 2019 high school graduates averaged 3.11 (NAEP High School Transcript Study). It is not a problem for many colleges, but it narrows your options at selective ones.

In high school

It meets the minimum at many four-year colleges, but you will be below the typical admitted student at selective ones. Improving your grades this year is worth the effort.

In college

In college it is under the 3.0 that many scholarships and graduate programs require, but well above the 2.0 needed for good standing.

Weighted or unweighted?

These figures are for the unweighted 4.0 scale. If your school gives extra points for Honors, AP or IB classes, your weighted GPA can be higher than 2.7. Colleges usually recalculate GPAs their own way, so they see both. Work out yours with the Weighted GPA calculator.

What a 2.7 GPA means for college

With a 2.7 you can get into many four-year colleges, especially regional public universities and less selective private colleges. Selective colleges are a stretch.

Colleges notice an upward trend, so better grades this year help. Community college followed by a transfer is another proven route.

What helps

  • Turn in everything: complete, on-time work is the quickest way to stop losing points.
  • Get help in your hardest class: tutoring or a study group early in the term.
  • Ask about retakes: some schools replace or average a retaken grade.

2.7 GPA – Frequently asked questions

How can I raise my 2.7 GPA? 

Now that you know what your GPA means, you may want to come up with a plan to raise your 2.7 GPA by a few points.      

  1. Ask for Help: If you’re just not getting the grades you hope for even though you’re working hard.  There’s no shame in asking for help.  Meet with your teacher, teacher’s aid or a tutor and ask they how they recommend you raise your grades. 
  2. Study First: Students have so much on their plates that it can be easy to push studying off until the last minute, but if raising your GPA is important to you make studying a priority.  If you study first, while your brain is fresh you’ll see great results.  
  3. Plan Accordingly: As you move forward, remember that you have some control over which classes you take.  Choose classes you enjoy and avoid unnecessarily difficult classes.  Choosing your class load wisely will give you a better chance at a high GPA.    

What does a 2.7 GPA indicate?

A 2.7 GPA indicates a 'B-' average, reflecting grades that are slightly below the 'B' range across various courses. It suggests that while you have an understanding of your coursework, there's significant scope for academic improvement.

Is a 2.7 GPA good enough for college admissions?

A 2.7 GPA can be sufficient for admissions into many colleges, particularly those that look at the overall application beyond just GPA. While it might not align with the criteria of more competitive colleges, an application enhanced by strong extracurricular involvement, personal achievements, and compelling recommendations can make a positive impact.

How can I improve a 2.7 GPA?

To enhance a 2.7 GPA, focus on achieving higher grades in future courses and consider retaking any courses where your performance was notably weak. Adopting more effective study habits, seeking additional academic support, and prioritizing your coursework can all contribute to improving your GPA.

What scholarship opportunities are available with a 2.7 GPA?

Scholarship opportunities for a 2.7 GPA may focus on aspects beyond academic merit, such as community service, leadership qualities, or specific talents. While highly competitive academic scholarships may be challenging to obtain with a 2.7 GPA, many programs recognize and reward personal growth, resilience, and contributions to community or extracurricular activities.
