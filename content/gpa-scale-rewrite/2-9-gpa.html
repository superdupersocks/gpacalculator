Is a 2.9 GPA good?

A 2.9 is a little below average. It means B work, about 84%, while 2019 high school graduates averaged 3.11 (NAEP High School Transcript Study). It is not a problem for many colleges, but it narrows your options at selective ones.

In high school

It meets the minimum at many four-year colleges, but you will be below the typical admitted student at selective ones. Improving your grades this year is worth the effort.

In college

In college it is under the 3.0 that many scholarships and graduate programs require, but well above the 2.0 needed for good standing.

Weighted or unweighted?

These figures are for the unweighted 4.0 scale. If your school gives extra points for Honors, AP or IB classes, your weighted GPA can be higher than 2.9. Colleges usually recalculate GPAs their own way, so they see both. Work out yours with the Weighted GPA calculator.

What a 2.9 GPA means for college

With a 2.9 you can get into many four-year colleges, especially regional public universities and less selective private colleges. Selective colleges are a stretch.

Colleges notice an upward trend, so better grades this year help. Community college followed by a transfer is another proven route.

What helps

Now that you know where you stand, it’s a good idea to think about ways to raise your GPA. Earning an above-average GPA takes commitment to your studies and completing work thoroughly, but with dedication a high GPA is within reach!

  1. Consistent Study Time: To improve your comprehension and develop a deep understanding of the course material, set aside a specific study time every day and stick to it. 
  2. Ask for Clarification: So you can avoid repeating the same mistakes and understand how to improve, reach out to your professor/teacher for clarification on any questions you don’t understand, or problems you got wrong on assignments, quizzes, and tests.
  3. Consider Your Class Schedule: It’s harder to maintain a high GPA with a course load full of exceptionally challenging classes. Instead, try choosing one class that is a big challenge and balancing it out with easier courses. 

2.9 GPA – Frequently asked questions

What does a 2.9 GPA indicate?

A 2.9 GPA signifies a 'B-' average, reflecting that you've been slightly below the average of a 'B' grade across your courses. It indicates you have a basic understanding of your coursework with potential areas for improvement.

Is a 2.9 GPA good enough for college admissions?

A 2.9 GPA is considered for college admissions at several institutions, especially those with a more holistic admissions process. While it may not meet the preferred criteria for more competitive colleges, a strong application can be bolstered by significant extracurricular achievements, compelling essays, and positive letters of recommendation.

How can I improve a 2.9 GPA?

To improve a 2.9 GPA, prioritize earning higher grades in future courses. Revisiting courses where you received lower grades and improving them can also help. Developing effective study habits, seeking academic support, and efficient time management are key strategies to enhance your GPA.

What scholarship opportunities are available with a 2.9 GPA?

With a 2.9 GPA, you may qualify for scholarships that focus on criteria beyond academic merit, such as community involvement, leadership roles, or specific talents and interests. While highly competitive scholarships may look for higher GPAs, there are still many opportunities for students with a 2.9 GPA to secure financial support for their education.
