Is a 3.2 GPA good?

A 3.2 is a solid GPA that sits right around the national average, which was 3.11 for 2019 high school graduates (NAEP High School Transcript Study). It works out to B+ work, about 87%.

In high school

Most colleges will consider it, especially with a challenging course load or an upward trend in your grades. Selective colleges usually expect more.

In college

In college it meets the 3.0 that many scholarships, graduate programs and employers use as a cutoff, so keep it there.

Weighted or unweighted?

These figures are for the unweighted 4.0 scale. If your school gives extra points for Honors, AP or IB classes, your weighted GPA can be higher than 3.2. Colleges usually recalculate GPAs their own way, so they see both. Work out yours with the Weighted GPA calculator.

What a 3.2 GPA means for college

A 3.2 is close to the middle of the range at many state universities and four-year colleges. Selective colleges usually look for higher, so they are reaches.

A strong senior year, a challenging course load and good test scores (if you send them) can make up ground. Compare your GPA with each college's published range.

What helps

Now that you know where you stand it’s a good idea to think about ways to raise your GPA. A 3.2 is above-average and raising it will take near-perfect work, but don’t be discouraged. With hard work you can raise your GPA from good to great! 

  1. Seek Deep Understanding: Complete all your assignments as thoroughly as possible and be sure you understand any concepts that trip you up.
  2. Go Above and Beyond: On large assignments or projects, go above and beyond the requirements to really showcase your skills and raise your grade! Raising your GPA takes hard work and it won’t happen overnight. Make a plan and stick to it, and you will see results! Commit yourself to turning in every assignment on time and completed in full.
  3. Stay On Track: Let your professor/teacher know your goals so they can help you stay on track and achieve!

3.2 GPA – Frequently asked questions

What does a 3.2 GPA indicate?

A 3.2 GPA signifies a solid 'B' average, reflecting steady academic performance. It shows you have a good understanding of your study materials and a consistent level of commitment to your academic duties.

Is a 3.2 GPA good enough for college admissions?

A 3.2 GPA is considered acceptable for college admissions at many institutions, indicating reliable academic achievement. While it may fall below the criteria for some more selective colleges, it can be part of a competitive application when combined with strong extracurricular achievements, compelling essays, and positive recommendations.

How can I improve a 3.2 GPA?

Improving a 3.2 GPA involves targeting higher grades in upcoming courses and possibly revisiting courses where you've scored lower, if permissible. Adopting effective study habits, leveraging academic support resources, and prioritizing time management are vital strategies for enhancing your GPA.

What scholarship opportunities are available with a 3.2 GPA?

A 3.2 GPA qualifies you for numerous scholarship opportunities, particularly those focused on academic merit and other criteria like community service or specific talents. While the most competitive scholarships might require higher GPAs, there are still plenty of scholarships for which a 3.2 GPA demonstrates sufficient academic diligence and potential.
