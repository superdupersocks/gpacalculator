A 3.3 GPA equals a B+ average, about 87–89%. It is a solid, above-average GPA that most colleges will view favorably, especially alongside good test scores and activities.

  • Letter grade: B+
  • Percentage: about 87–89%
  • National average: 3.11 for 2019 high school graduates (NAEP High School Transcript Study)
  • Scale: unweighted 4.0

Is a 3.3 GPA good?

Yes. A 3.3 is a good GPA: B+ work, about 87–89%, and comfortably above the national average of 3.11 for 2019 graduates (NAEP High School Transcript Study).

In high school

It shows steady, above-average work and keeps a wide range of four-year colleges open. Strong courses and an upward trend make it even more convincing.

In college

In college it keeps you in good standing for most scholarships and clears the 3.0 that many graduate programs and employers ask for.

Weighted or unweighted?

These figures are for the unweighted 4.0 scale. If your school gives extra points for Honors, AP or IB classes, your weighted GPA can be higher than 3.3. Colleges usually recalculate GPAs their own way, so they see both. Work out yours with the Weighted GPA calculator.

What a 3.3 GPA means for college

A 3.3 fits the typical range at a large number of four-year colleges and is competitive at many selective ones. The most selective schools usually admit students with higher GPAs, so treat them as reaches.

Check each college's published GPA range (often on its admissions page or Common Data Set) and build a balanced list around it.

What helps

  • Find the weakest class: moving one B+ to an A− lifts your GPA more than polishing classes you already ace.
  • Use office hours: ask teachers what separates your work from an A.
  • Plan ahead: keep a calendar of tests and deadlines so nothing is done last-minute.

3.3 GPA – Frequently asked questions

How can I raise my 3.3 GPA? 

Now that you know where you stand you can make a plan to raise your GPA and achieve your goals. A 3.3 is above-average and raising it will take near-perfect work, but don’t be discouraged. With hard work and determination you can do it! 

  1. Dive into Assignments: Complete all your assignments as thoroughly as possible and ask for clarification on anything that you don’t fully understand.  
  2. Tackle Common Mistakes: Look back through past work and tests to see what kind of errors you often make.  Once you recognize what kind of errors you often make you’ll be able to watch out for and avoid them in the future
  3. Ask for Advice: Take some time to sit down with your teachers and let them know your goals.  Teachers are always happy to help motivated students achieve their educational goals!

What does a 3.3 GPA indicate?

A 3.3 GPA reflects a 'B+' average, signaling consistent above-average academic performance. It demonstrates a good grasp of your coursework and a commendable level of dedication to your studies.

Is a 3.3 GPA good enough for college admissions?

A 3.3 GPA is favorable for college admissions to a variety of institutions, indicating solid academic performance. While it might be below the average for highly selective universities, it can support a strong application alongside robust extracurricular activities, personal statements, and letters of recommendation.

How can I improve a 3.3 GPA?

To improve a 3.3 GPA, aim to achieve higher grades in future courses and consider retaking any courses where your grades were lower. Emphasizing effective study techniques, seeking academic support, and managing your time efficiently are key to raising your GPA.

What scholarship opportunities are available with a 3.3 GPA?

With a 3.3 GPA, you're eligible for a wide range of scholarships, especially those targeting academic merit. While the most competitive scholarships may require higher GPAs, many organizations and institutions offer scholarships for students with GPAs that demonstrate strong academic potential and commitment.
