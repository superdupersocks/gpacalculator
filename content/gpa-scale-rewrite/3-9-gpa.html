Is a 3.9 GPA good?

Yes. A 3.9 is an excellent GPA: it means A work across your classes, about 94%, and it is well above the national average. High school graduates in 2019 averaged 3.11, according to the NAEP High School Transcript Study.

In high school

Admissions officers will read it as consistent, high-level work. At this level the rest of your application (course rigor, test scores if you send them, essays, activities) decides the most competitive admissions.

In college

In college a GPA this high stands out for scholarships, honors programs and graduate school, where 3.0 to 3.5 is a common minimum.

What a 3.9 GPA means for college

A 3.9 makes you a competitive applicant at most colleges, including many selective ones. At the most selective schools most admitted students have a GPA this high or higher, so your courses, essays and activities carry the decision.

Apply to a balanced list: a few reach schools, several where your GPA is at or above their typical range, and a couple of likely admits.

Freshman

If you are a freshman, your GPA tends to change before your application is sent to the college but having a 3.9 GPA is a commendable feat. It is a sign that you are performing excellent and acquired high grades in most of your classes, and the next few years could see you perform as a better student and make a good impression on colleges with your academic development. As a freshman, if you can retain or go beyond the 3.9 GPA, you will be admitted by various selective schools. You may not have chosen colleges yet, and that's fine: use our GPA calculator each term to see where your GPA is heading.

Sophomore

Having passed through the first two years of high school with a robust GPA is an exceptional achievement. It is likely for your GPA to change slightly before you find admission to the college in your senior year but there will be no much difference if you continue to study at the same pace. Most of the best schools will consider you if you have a 3.9 GPA and this will offer you a range of choices when you want to select the college you want to study in. Junior

With a 3.9 GPA, you have succeeded in getting to the college application time. However, your GPA will not be changed at this point, and you should be applying to colleges with a GPA close to the value of a 3.9. It indicates that you would be counting on your eligibility to be admitted by various selective colleges. Senior

This is your senior year, and there are chances that you have applied to various schools with your GPA. The process of getting admitted into schools should not be a problem for you if you have maintained a strong GPA throughout your high school except in rare cases where some schools are applying stringent measures. What helps

It can be very difficult to raise a 3.9 GPA but if you’re really determined here are a few things you might try.  

  1. Review Your Mistakes: Students often make the same types of mistakes again and again.  Look back through past assignments and see what types of mistakes you tend to make.  Then you’ll be able to keep an eye out for them in the future.
  2. Give Time to Every Assignment: Each and every grade counts when you’re working to raise a 3.9 GPA.  Be sure to give every single assignment, even easy ones, the time they deserve.
  3. Go Above and Beyond: Ask your professors/teachers for extra work to build deep understanding and maybe even earn some extra credit!

3.9 GPA – Frequently asked questions

What does a 3.9 GPA signify?

A 3.9 GPA indicates exceptionally high academic performance, demonstrating that you have achieved 'A' grades in almost all of your classes, reflecting a strong commitment to academic excellence.

How close is a 3.9 GPA to perfect?

A 3.9 GPA is very close to a perfect 4.0 GPA. It suggests that you have consistently earned high grades, with the majority being 'A's, and possibly a few 'A-' grades or one 'B+'.

Can I improve from a 3.9 to a 4.0 GPA?

Improving from a 3.9 to a 4.0 GPA is achievable but requires focused effort. Excelling in all remaining courses or retaking courses where you scored lower, if your school's policies permit, can help you reach a 4.0.

Is a 3.9 GPA competitive for top universities?

Yes, a 3.9 GPA is highly competitive for admission into top universities. It showcases your academic capabilities, though top universities also evaluate extracurricular activities, essays, and recommendations for a comprehensive assessment.

Will a 3.9 GPA qualify for scholarships?

Indeed, a 3.9 GPA qualifies you for numerous academic merit-based scholarships. It signals to scholarship committees your dedication and success in your studies.

How does a 3.9 GPA impact job prospects after graduation?

A 3.9 GPA positively influences job prospects, signaling to potential employers your diligence and ability to excel. However, employers also value relevant experience, skills, and how well you fit the job role.

Can I round up my 3.9 GPA to 4.0 on my resume or applications?

Rounding up a 3.9 GPA to 4.0 on applications or resumes is not recommended. Honesty is key, so it's best to state your exact GPA to maintain integrity and credibility.
