This policy explains what information GPACalculator.net collects, how it's used and the choices you have. It applies to everyone who visits the site.

Who we are

GPACalculator.net is run by Calcadia LLC, based in California. For any privacy question or request, email team@gpacalculator.net.

Information we collect

What you enter in a calculator

You don't need an account, a name or an email address to use our calculators. Most of them work entirely in your browser. Some let you save your classes and grades so you can come back later; that information is stored on your own device (in your browser's storage), not on our servers. You can delete it at any time with the calculator's reset button or by clearing your browser's site data.

Messages you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to improve the site.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our servers record basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the time of the request. We use it to run the site and keep it secure.

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used: which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are (country or city level), what devices they use, and whether a calculator was used. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

The site is free because it shows ads. Our advertising partner, Publisher First, Inc. dba Freestar, and the ad networks it works with, including Google, use cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure how ads perform and, where allowed, show ads based on your interests. These partners collect this information directly under their own privacy policies:

  • Freestar's privacy policy
  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services; you can manage Google ad personalization in My Ad Center
  • Industry opt-out pages: optout.aboutads.info (US) and youronlinechoices.eu (EU/UK)

Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent to these cookies through the consent banner. You can change your choices at any time with the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link at the bottom of every page.

How we use information

  • to run the site and its calculators, and fix problems;
  • to understand which pages and tools are useful, so we can improve them;
  • to show ads that keep the site free;
  • to keep the site secure and prevent abuse;
  • to answer your messages.

Sharing

We don't sell your name, email address or anything you type into a calculator. Information is shared with the service providers that help us run the site (such as our hosting provider and Google Analytics), with the advertising partners described above, and when the law requires it. Under some state laws, letting ad partners collect information through cookies for personalized ads counts as "selling" or "sharing" personal information; you can opt out with the link at the bottom of every page.

Your choices and rights

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, use the opt-out links above, and change your ad choices with the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link.

California and other US states. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we have about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

European Economic Area and the UK. You may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, to restrict or object to how it's used, and to withdraw consent at any time. We rely on your consent for advertising and analytics cookies where consent is required, and on our legitimate interest in running and securing the site for server logs. Our partners may process data in the United States. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email team@gpacalculator.net. We may need to confirm it's you before we act on it.

Children

Our tools are made for students, parents and teachers, and you don't have to give us any personal information to use them. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you're under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before emailing us. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

How long we keep information

We keep emails only as long as we need them to help you. Server logs and analytics data are kept for a limited time and then deleted. Ad partners keep data according to their own policies.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the site and the information it handles, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS). No website can be completely secure, though.

Links to other sites

We link to other websites, such as colleges and government data sources. Their privacy practices are their own, so please read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we'll change the date at the top. Our Terms of Use also apply to your use of the site.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email team@gpacalculator.net.
