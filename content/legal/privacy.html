This policy explains what information GPACalculator.net collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. GPACalculator.net is run by Calcadia LLC.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and our calculators don't ask for your name or email.
  • The grades and classes you enter stay in your browser. They aren't sent to us.
  • We show ads and use analytics. Our partners use cookies to do that, and you can opt out.
  • We don't sell the information you type into our calculators.

What you enter in our calculators

Our calculators run in your browser. Some save your classes and grades in your browser's local storage, so they're still there when you come back. That data stays on your device; we don't receive it. You can clear it at any time with the calculator's clear button or by clearing your browser's site data.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our servers and partners receive standard technical information when you visit: your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, the page that referred you, and the time of your visit.

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, such as which pages are popular and where people get stuck. Google Analytics uses cookies and reports this to us in aggregate. You can opt out with Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

Advertising

The site is free because it shows ads. Our ads are managed by Freestar (Publisher First, Inc. dba Freestar), which works with advertising partners, including Google. These partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure results, and, where allowed, show ads based on your interests. Freestar explains how it handles data in its privacy policy.

You can manage Google's ad personalization at Google's ad settings, and opt out of interest-based ads from many companies at YourAdChoices or the Network Advertising Initiative.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you and keep them only as long as we need to.

Children's privacy

The site is meant for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your California privacy rights

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act gives you the right to know what personal information is collected about you, to ask us to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. Advertising cookies on this site can count as sharing under that law. To opt out, use the Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information link at the bottom of every page. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

If you're in the EEA or the UK

If you visit from the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent before we or our partners use advertising and analytics cookies, and you can change your choice at any time through the privacy settings link on the site. We rely on that consent for ad and analytics cookies, and on our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website for standard server logs.

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to receive a copy of it, to restrict or object to how it's used, and to withdraw consent at any time. To use these rights, email us. Our partners may process information in the United States, which has different data protection laws from yours. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Your choices

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.
  • Opt out of interest-based ads using the links above.
  • Clear saved calculator data by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Links to other sites

We link to colleges, government data sources and other websites. Their privacy practices are their own, so please read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we'll change the date at the top.
