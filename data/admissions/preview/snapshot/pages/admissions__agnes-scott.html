Agnes Scott College Average GPA & Admissions

Agnes Scott College is a private nonprofit 4-year college in Decatur, Georgia, with 843 undergraduates. It admitted 62% of first-year applicants for fall 2024.

  • Acceptance rate: 62% for fall 2024
  • Average high school GPA: 3.63, as reported by the college for 2025–26
  • SAT, middle 50%: 620–695 reading and writing, 540–635 math
  • ACT, middle 50%: 25–31 composite
  • Average net price: $25,609 a year (2022–23)

Agnes Scott College average GPA

Agnes Scott College reported an average high school GPA of 3.63 for its first-year students, in its 2025–26 Common Data Set. The college doesn't say whether it's weighted or unweighted.

Agnes Scott College acceptance rate

Agnes Scott College admitted 1,299 of 2,090 first-year applicants for fall 2024, an acceptance rate of 62%, according to what it reported to the U.S. Department of Education.

AP credit

Agnes Scott College awards college credit for Advanced Placement (AP) exams, according to what it reported to the U.S. Department of Education for 2024–25. The college decides which exams and scores earn credit.

It doesn't award credit for life experience.

Average net price

First-time, full-time undergraduates who received grant or scholarship aid paid an average net price of $25,609 a year at Agnes Scott College in 2022–23, according to the U.S. Department of Education.

Net price is the full cost of attendance minus grants and scholarships.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average high school GPA at Agnes Scott College?

The average high school GPA of Agnes Scott College's first-year students who submitted one is 3.63, as reported by the college in its 2025–26 Common Data Set. Colleges calculate GPA in different ways, so this average can't be compared directly with your own GPA. Source: Agnes Scott College Common Data Set 2025–26.

What is the acceptance rate at Agnes Scott College?

Agnes Scott College's acceptance rate was 62% for fall 2024: it admitted 1,299 of 2,090 first-year applicants, according to what it reported to the U.S. Department of Education (IPEDS).

What SAT scores do Agnes Scott College students have?

The middle 50% of Agnes Scott College's fall 2024 first-year students who submitted SAT scores (27% of them) scored 620–695 in Reading and Writing and 540–635 in Math. The median scores were 660 (Reading and Writing) and 580 (Math).

What ACT scores do Agnes Scott College students have?

The middle 50% of Agnes Scott College's fall 2024 first-year students who submitted ACT scores (17% of them) had composite scores of 25–31, with a median of 27.

Does Agnes Scott College accept AP credit?

Yes. Agnes Scott College awards college credit for Advanced Placement (AP) exams, according to what it reported to the U.S. Department of Education for 2024–25. The college decides which exams and scores earn credit.

What is the average net price at Agnes Scott College?

In 2022–23, first-time, full-time undergraduates who received grant or scholarship aid paid an average net price of $25,609 a year at Agnes Scott College, according to the U.S. Department of Education (IPEDS). Net price is the cost of attendance minus grants and scholarships.
