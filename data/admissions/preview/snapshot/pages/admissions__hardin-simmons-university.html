Admission Requirements

High School GPA Required

Your cumulative high school grade point average.

High School Record Required

Your transcript: the courses you took and the grades you earned.

High School Class Rank Considered if submitted

Your ranking relative to other students in your graduating class.

College Preparatory Program Considered if submitted

Completion of a college preparatory curriculum in high school.

Recommendations Considered if submitted

Letters of recommendation from teachers, counselors, or other mentors.

Essay or Personal Statement Considered if submitted

A personal statement or essay written for your application.

Test Scores Test optional

SAT or ACT scores.

Other Tests Test blind

Other admission tests, such as the Wonderlic or WISC-III.

Demonstration of Competencies Considered if submitted

Evidence of specific skills through portfolios, auditions, or other demonstrations.

Work Experience Not considered

Paid or volunteer work.

Legacy Status Considered if submitted

Whether a parent or other relative attended the college.

Frequently Asked Questions

Hardin Simmons University requires a high school GPA to be considered for admission, according to what it reported to the U.S. Department of Education for fall 2024. Federal data doesn't include GPA averages, and we haven't found one published by Hardin Simmons University itself, so we don't list an average GPA.

Hardin Simmons University's acceptance rate was 90% for fall 2024: it admitted 3,131 of 3,479 first-year applicants, according to what it reported to the U.S. Department of Education (IPEDS).

The middle 50% of Hardin Simmons University's fall 2024 first-year students who submitted SAT scores (32% of them) scored 510–600 in Reading and Writing and 500–590 in Math. The median scores were 560 (Reading and Writing) and 540 (Math).

The middle 50% of Hardin Simmons University's fall 2024 first-year students who submitted ACT scores (20% of them) had composite scores of 19–26, with a median of 22.

Yes. Hardin Simmons University awards college credit for Advanced Placement (AP) exams, according to what it reported to the U.S. Department of Education for 2024–25. The college decides which exams and scores earn credit.

In 2022–23, first-time, full-time undergraduates who received grant or scholarship aid paid an average net price of $21,321 a year at Hardin Simmons University, according to the U.S. Department of Education (IPEDS). Net price is the cost of attendance minus grants and scholarships.
